# VISION - HYMN REMAKER

## THE ULTIMATE GOAL
To create a fully autonomous, high-fidelity production house for religious and classical music modernization. The platform should take any form of musical input (MIDI, MXL, Image, Audio) and produce a professional-grade music video with synchronized lyrics, AI-driven vocals, and dynamic cinematic visuals.

## CORE PRINCIPLES
1. **Accessibility:** Preserve traditional hymns by making them accessible and engaging for modern audiences.
2. **Autonomy:** The system should operate with minimal human intervention, from ingestion to publication.
3. **Quality:** Every output must meet high standards for audio fidelity and visual aesthetic.
4. **Interactivity:** Provide a "Producer's Dashboard" where humans can guide AI creativity without being bogged down by technical details.

## TARGET AUDIENCE
- Church media departments.
- Content creators for social platforms (TikTok/YouTube).
- Ambient/Lofi music listeners.
- Educational institutions.
